## URLs and notes

- **`east-evans-creek/census_blocks`**: <https://tigerweb.geo.census.gov/arcgis/rest/services/TIGERweb/Tracts_Blocks/MapServer/12/query>  
  TIGERweb layer 'Census 2020 > Census Blocks', fields POP100 and HU100, every block touching the study window (310 blocks).
- **`east-evans-creek/hls_true_colour`**: <https://wvs.earthdata.nasa.gov/api/v1/snapshot?REQUEST=GetSnapshot&TIME=2026-07-15&BBOX=42.53993,-123.08683,42.71998,-122.79872&CRS=EPSG:4326&LAYERS=HLS_L30_Nadir_BRDF_Adjusted_Reflectance&FORMAT=image/jpeg&WIDTH=1574&HEIGHT=1334>  
  NASA Worldview Snapshots (GIBS). The haziest of 10 usable views in the 21 days after ignition; 37% of it is smoke or cloud.
- **`east-evans-creek/nasadem_n42w123`**: <https://data.lpdaac.earthdatacloud.nasa.gov/lp-prod-protected/NASADEM_HGT.001/NASADEM_HGT_n42w123/NASADEM_HGT_n42w123.zip>  
  Short name NASADEM_HGT v001 at NASA LP DAAC, doi:10.5067/MEaSUREs/NASADEM/NASADEM_HGT.001.
- **`east-evans-creek/nasadem_n42w124`**: <https://data.lpdaac.earthdatacloud.nasa.gov/lp-prod-protected/NASADEM_HGT.001/NASADEM_HGT_n42w124/NASADEM_HGT_n42w124.zip>  
  Short name NASADEM_HGT v001 at NASA LP DAAC, doi:10.5067/MEaSUREs/NASADEM/NASADEM_HGT.001.
- **`east-evans-creek/nisar_gcov`**: <https://search.asf.alaska.edu/#/?dataset=NISAR&productTypes=GCOV>  
  Short names NISAR_L2_GCOV_V1, NISAR_L2_GCOV_PROVISIONAL_V1 at the Alaska Satellite Facility DAAC (Earthdata login). Only the study window of the HV layer was read from each ~5 GB file (57 MB kept in data/work/). Granules: NISAR_L2_PR_GCOV_023_135_A_023_4005_DHDH_A_20260623T131901_20260623T131936_P05023_N_F_J_001; NISAR_L2_PR_GCOV_024_135_A_023_4005_DHDH_A_20260705T131900_20260705T131935_P05023_N_F_J_001; NISAR_L2_PR_GCOV_032_135_A_023_4005_DHDH_A_20261009T131857_20261009T131932_P05023_N_F_J_001; NISAR_L2_PR_GCOV_033_135_A_023_4005_DHDH_A_20261021T131857_20261021T131932_P05023_N_F_J_001
- **`east-evans-creek/opera_dist_alert_s1`**: <https://search.asf.alaska.edu/#/?dataset=OPERA-S1&productTypes=DIST-ALERT-S1>  
  55 granules over the study window, acquired 2026-01-02 to 2026-02-26; 0 acquisition dates on or after ignition (2026-07-10). Until the archive reaches the fire, the scar is drawn from OPERA RTC-S1, the backscatter product DIST-ALERT-S1 is itself made from.
- **`east-evans-creek/opera_rtc_s1`**: <https://search.asf.alaska.edu/#/?dataset=OPERA-S1&productTypes=RTC>  
  Short name OPERA_L2_RTC-S1_V1 at the Alaska Satellite Facility DAAC (Earthdata login). VH, VV and mask layers of 238 burst files on tracks 013, 035, 115, 137; only the study window was read (261 MB kept in data/work/east-evans-creek/rtc/).
- **`east-evans-creek/osm`**: <https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter>  
  Used to label blocks with a road and a nearby place, and to measure road length inside mud paths.
- **`east-evans-creek/usa_structures`**: <https://services2.arcgis.com/FiaPA4ga0iQKduv3/arcgis/rest/services/USA_Structures_View/FeatureServer/0>  
  14928 buildings in and around the study window; occupancy class and centre point used.
- **`east-evans-creek/usgs_pwfdf`**: <https://www.sciencebase.gov/catalog/item/6af460821ba49b1bd080d887>  
  Looked up by fire name in https://earthquake.usgs.gov/arcgis/rest/services/ls/pwfdf/MapServer (layer 0). Assessment dated 2026-11-11; fire start 2026-07-10. Shapefiles: perimeter, 150 basins, 1206 stream segments. Part of the PWFDF collection, doi:10.5066/P14EWYME.
